# 🎮 ESCAPE.EXE

> At 3:07 AM, Pip — the sprout hero of Sam's buggy game "Super Jump Quest 3" — glitches out of his game and falls onto Sam's desktop.

[![Play on Web](https://img.shields.io/badge/▶_Play_On_Web-Play_Now_on_Itch.io-2ea44f?style=for-the-badge&logo=itch.io)](https://ayesha-siddiqa.itch.io/escapeexe)

## 📖 About the Game
Sam thinks he's a bug. You're the only one who knows he isn't.

Drag files into stairs, collect the letters H-E-L-P, escape the Recycle Bin before the timer runs out, and get Sam to notice Pip before it's too late. But be careful what you restore...

* **3 Endings:** Happy, Sad, and... Unexpected.
* **Made for:** SHIFT Game Jam Abu Dhabi 2026 — Theme: *"Between Two Worlds"*.

---

## 🕹️ Controls
* **A / D** — Move
* **Space** — Jump
* **Mouse** — Drag files, click pop-ups and buttons
